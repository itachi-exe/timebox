How It Works

Five steps.
One sealed future.

From your first word to tamper-proof permanence in under two minutes. No accounts. No recovery phrases. Just your wallet and the date.

01
Connect Wallet

Sui wallet (Phantom or Suiet). No signup. No email. Your wallet address is your account, identity, and recovery key.

Sui · Wallet-First
02
Write & Seal

Write your message. Attach a photo. Pick a reveal date. Pay a small WAL fee. This covers Walrus storage upfront.

WAL Fee
03
Walrus Stores It

Encrypted, uploaded to Walrus as a blob. A cryptographic blob ID is returned. If one byte changes, the ID changes. Locked.

Walrus Blob
04
Sui Records It

A Move contract records the blob ID and unlock timestamp on Sui. Tatum RPC handles the transaction. Time-lock enforced by code.

Tatum RPC
05
Reveal Day

Connect the same wallet. The contract checks the timestamp. Walrus serves the blob. Your message, exactly as you wrote it.

Walrus Verified

Tech Stack

Every piece is load-bearing.

Walrus, Tatum, and Sui are not decoration. Each one is the reason the next layer works.

Walrus
Decentralized Vault

All capsule content stored as encrypted blobs. Walrus returns a blob ID, a cryptographic fingerprint. One changed byte = different ID.

Tatum
Sui RPC Layer

Every on-chain action (wallet connection, WAL fee, contract write, query) runs through Tatum's Sui RPC endpoints.

Sui
Time-Lock Enforcer

A Move contract on Sui Mainnet holds the blob ID and unlock timestamp. One rule: key not released until epoch passes the unlock date.
